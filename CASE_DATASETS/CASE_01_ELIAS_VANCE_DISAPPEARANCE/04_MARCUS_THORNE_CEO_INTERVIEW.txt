INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT - WITNESS 01
Date: October 15, 2026
Subject: Marcus Thorne, CEO Nexus BioTech

DETECTIVE: Mr. Thorne, when was the last time you saw Elias Vance?

THORNE: Yesterday morning. He seemed erratic. Elias has been under a lot of stress. I told him to take a few days off, but he refused.

DETECTIVE: Do you know why his car was found abandoned on Route 9?

THORNE: Look, Elias had access to our most sensitive intellectual property. The quantum-computing blueprints. I checked the server logs this morning. He downloaded the entire repository onto an external drive at 9:00 PM last night. He's a corporate spy, Detective. He sold us out and ran.

DETECTIVE: Where were you last night between 9:00 PM and 11:00 PM?

THORNE: I was at the Mayor's charity gala. Julianne Reed, my Head of Security, was my plus-one. We were there all evening. You can check the guest list.

DETECTIVE: Did Ms. Reed ever leave the gala?

THORNE: No. She was by my side the entire night.
