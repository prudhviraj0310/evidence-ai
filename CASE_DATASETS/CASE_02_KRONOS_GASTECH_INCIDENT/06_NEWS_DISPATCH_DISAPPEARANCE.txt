REUTERS DISPATCH // KRONOS ISLAND INCIDENT
Date: January 18, 2014

ABILA, Tethys — Authorities on the island of Kronos report the sudden disappearance of multiple senior executives from GAStech headquarters following the company's tenth-anniversary gala celebration.

According to local police, at approximately 21:30 yesterday evening, power was abruptly severed across the northern quadrant of the Abila corporate campus. When emergency backup generators engaged, two executive transport vans and seven staff members were unaccounted for.

Investigators are scrutinizing internal email headers and vehicle GPS telemetry logs from the company fleet. Security chief Loreto Bodrogi claimed his entire unit remained stationed at the main security post until 23:00, but electronic badge records and perimeter CCTV show unauthorized vehicle dispatches through Gate 3 during the blackout window.
